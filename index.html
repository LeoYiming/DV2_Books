04

What Does This Tell Us?

Malaysia's book landscape is changing across publishing, digital consumption and physical access. Together, these patterns connect changes in what is published, who engages with digital books and where physical bookstores are found.

Publishing is changing

Book categories and subject areas have shifted over the past decade, while digital publishing has become a more visible part of total publication applications.

Digital activity is concentrated

Registered e-Sentral users are concentrated among younger adults, while lower-value transactions make up the largest share of e-book purchases.

Place still matters

Bookstore records cluster around major urban areas, while population-adjusted comparisons reveal a different pattern from raw bookstore totals alone.
